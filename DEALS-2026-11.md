# BulkApparel deals, checked Nov 18, 2026

Checked on [bulkapparel.com](https://www.bulkapparel.com) in Chrome as a guest (the site was not signed in), with a test cart of Comfort Colors 1717 white M at $6.74. Nothing was ordered, no account was made, and the cart was emptied afterwards.

## 1. Does 10% off stack with free shipping?

**Yes. They stack. One does not cancel the other.**

- **Free shipping starts at $79.** Under $79, shipping starts at $6.85. Source: [bulkapparel.com/shipping](https://www.bulkapparel.com/shipping) and the site-wide banner.
- **The 10% is the automatic bulk discount on orders of $1,000 or more.** No code needed. Tiers: 5% over $250, 7% over $500, 10% over $1,000, 12% over $2,500, 14% over $5,000. Source: banner and [bulkapparel.com/help](https://www.bulkapparel.com/help) ("Any additional discounts? Volume Discounts").
- **Cart test:** 160 shirts, subtotal $1,078.40, "Bulk Discount -$107.84", total $970.56, and the cart still read "Free Shipping Activated!".
- **The $79 is counted before discounts.** With 12 shirts ($80.88) and a 7% code the total fell to $75.22 and both the cart and the checkout page still showed "Free Shipping activated!". At 11 shirts ($74.14) the cart asked for $4.86 more, which is $79 minus the subtotal, not the discounted total.
- Not seen: the final shipping dollar line at checkout, because it needs a street address. NH ZIP 03301 showed $0.00 tax.
