# BulkApparel deals, checked Black Friday, Nov 27, 2026

Checked on [bulkapparel.com](https://www.bulkapparel.com) in Chrome as a guest between 9:10 and 9:30 am Eastern, with a test cart of Comfort Colors 1717 white M at $6.74. Nothing was ordered, no account was made, and the cart was emptied afterwards. Baseline: [DEALS-2026-11.md](DEALS-2026-11.md) (Nov 18).

## What changed since Nov 18

- **New code GRATEFUL10: 10% off any size order.** It is the Thanksgiving code ("Happy Thanksgiving! Enjoy 10% OFF Sitewide Today Only!"), and it still worked this morning. Source: the banner on every page and the cart test below.
- **That makes today better than Nov 18 for any order under $1,000,** by 3 to 10 points depending on size. At $1,000 and up nothing changed: the bulk discount still gives 10% or more and codes are refused.
- **No Black Friday sale of its own.** The Black Friday page is still the "wishlist countdown" with no prices or code. Source: [bulkapparel.com/build-black-friday-wishlist](https://www.bulkapparel.com/build-black-friday-wishlist).
- **Not changed:** 1717 prices, the $79 free shipping line, the bulk tiers, the excluded styles, the 10 sale styles, and AUTUMN7 (still works, 7%).

## 1. Does the Black Friday offer stack with free shipping and the bulk discount?

**With free shipping: yes. With the bulk discount: no.**

- **Free shipping still starts at $79, counted before discounts.** With 12 shirts ($80.88) and GRATEFUL10 the total fell to $72.79 and both the cart and the checkout page still read "Free Shipping activated!". At 11 shirts ($74.14) the cart asked for $4.86 more, which is $79 minus the subtotal. Source: cart test and [bulkapparel.com/shipping](https://www.bulkapparel.com/shipping).
- **Codes do not stack with the bulk discount.** On the $1,078.40 cart the site said "Coupon GRATEFUL10 cannot be applied with bulk discount", and the same for AUTUMN7. The total was $970.56, the same as Nov 18. Source: cart test and [bulkapparel.com/help#content-75](https://www.bulkapparel.com/help#content-75) ("Only one discount or promotional offer can be applied per order").
- **Codes do not stack with each other.** With AUTUMN7 on the cart, GRATEFUL10 was refused ("cannot be used in conjunction with other coupons").
- **Under $1,000 the code replaces the bulk discount, and that is the better deal.** With the code on the cart the bulk discount is not added; the 10% code applies alone.
- **Bulk tiers are the same:** 5% over $250, 7% over $500, 10% over $1,000, 12% over $2,500, 14% over $5,000. Seen in the cart without a code: $256.12 gave -$12.81 (5%), $997.52 gave -$69.83 (7%), $1,078.40 gave -$107.84 (10%).
- Not seen: the final shipping dollar line at checkout, because it needs a street address. Tax showed $0.00.

## 3. Which one to use by order size

- **Under $1,000: GRATEFUL10.** 10% instead of nothing (under $250), 5% ($250 to $499) or 7% ($500 to $999).
- **$1,000 to $2,499: no code needed.** The bulk discount gives the same 10% by itself, and the code is refused.
- **$2,500 and up: bulk discount** (12%, then 14% at $5,000).
- **How to enter it:** type the code in "Have a promo code?" in the cart. If the cart is over $250 the site says "Code successfully applied" but keeps showing the bulk discount line until the page is reloaded; after a reload the code shows in its place.

## 4. Other things checked

- **GRATEFUL10 could stop at any time.** The banner calls it "Today Only" for Thanksgiving, and it was still live the morning after. If the order is under $1,000, sooner is safer.
- **AUTUMN7 (7%) still works** and is still listed on [bulkapparel.com/kickoff20](https://www.bulkapparel.com/kickoff20), but GRATEFUL10 beats it at every size.
- **Sale items:** the same 10 styles on [bulkapparel.com/sales](https://www.bulkapparel.com/sales) (Gildan, Hanes, Port & Company, Sport-Tek). Comfort Colors 1717 is not one of them.
- **Excluded from all discounts:** white Gildan G500 and G185, white Bella+Canvas 3001, white Hanes 5280. White 1717 is not excluded; the code took 10% off it in the test.
- **No sale popup** appeared on the home page, only the banner.
